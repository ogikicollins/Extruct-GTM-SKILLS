# InteractOne — eCommerce SEO Service GTM Strategy (v2, research-backed)

---

## 0. Situation in one paragraph

InteractOne is a 27-year, ~18-person, 100% onshore Cincinnati eCommerce agency (founder/CEO Brian Dwyer) that just repositioned to **"B2B commerce built to perform"** for manufacturers and distributors, but has not built demand-gen around it. The SEO service is strong and differentiated (dev + programmatic SEO + B2B/industrial catalog fluency + senior-only delivery) but under-marketed, unpriced publicly, and capacity-constrained. The opportunity is a **narrow, high-intent outbound motion** that monetizes the new positioning without overwhelming a small team.

---

## 1. What we are actually selling (offer strategy)

Do **not** sell "SEO." Sell one sharp thing:

> **Organic traffic recovery and large-catalog programmatic SEO for B2B and industrial eCommerce on Adobe Commerce and BigCommerce — done by an onshore senior team that also writes the code.**

Two sub-offers, one list:
- **Recovery:** "Your replatform cost you 20–40% of organic traffic and nobody owns the fix." (Aladdin Temp-Rite proof: +66% total / +25% organic.)
- **Scale:** "You have 8,000 product and category pages and 400 of them rank." Programmatic SEO is the capability competitors can't cheaply copy.

Positioning line: *"The SEO team that reads your Magento logs, not just your blog."*

### Fix before launch (gaps from research)
1. **Productize the entry offer.** Free *Programmatic SEO Teardown* (Loom) → fixed-fee *Technical SEO & Migration Audit* ($3,500–$7,500) → retainer.
2. **Add a light AEO / AI-search line** (schema, entity optimization, answer-formatted content) at +$900–$2,500/mo. It's a 2026 buyer question and a competitor wedge.
3. **Refresh proof.** Pull updated 90-day numbers from 3 long-tenured clients; instrument every new engagement for a results story.

## 2. ICP (beachhead)

| Dimension | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Business type | B2B / industrial / MRO / manufacturer / distributor / wholesale eComm | Matches InteractOne's repositioning + differentiation |
| Revenue | $10M–$250M | Budget for $5–15K/mo, not big enough for an in-house SEO team |
| Catalog | 5,000+ SKUs | Programmatic SEO only matters at scale; it's the qualifier |
| Platform | Adobe Commerce / Magento, BigCommerce (secondary: Shopify Plus) | Where InteractOne has partner status + dev depth |
| Geography | US | Onshore pitch only lands with US buyers |
| In-house SEO | None or one overloaded marketer | "Senior crew, no juniors" resonates with lean teams |
| Health signal | Organic flat/declining, or replatformed in last 12 months | Intent |

**Disqualify:** DTC fashion/beauty with small catalogs, sub-$5M revenue, agencies, 3+ person in-house SEO teams, non-US.

**TAM sketch:** US manufacturers/distributors doing eCommerce on Adobe Commerce or BigCommerce with 5k+ SKUs ≈ 3,000–6,000 companies. Reachable, tight, ownable.

## 3. Trigger events (list-build priority order)

1. **Replatform / migration in last 6–12 months** — organic drops 20–40%, unowned. Highest intent. Detect via BuiltWith platform-change history, press, "we've moved" banners, changelog of theme.
2. **Organic visibility down >20% YoY** (Ahrefs / Semrush trend) with flat or rising paid spend.
3. **New Head of eCommerce / Digital / Marketing** (last 90 days) — new budget, wants a fast win. LinkedIn job-change filter.
4. **Open req for SEO / Digital Marketing Manager** — they feel the gap, haven't filled it. (Also: they may hire instead of buy — time the outreach before the req closes.)
5. **Site redesign / relaunch announced.**
6. **Google core-update losers** — third-party loss lists after each update.
7. **Aging Magento 1 / early Magento 2** — SEO entry now, migration expansion later (InteractOne is Adobe Bronze partner).

## 4. Personas and messaging

| Persona | Pain | Hook | Proof to cite |
|---|---|---|---|
| VP / Director eCommerce | Organic revenue flat while paid CAC climbs | "Reduce dependence on Google Ads" | BASCO +73% revenue |
| Marketing Director / Head of Digital | 1–3 person team can't touch thousands of pages | Programmatic SEO does it at scale | "8,000 pages, 400 ranking" |
| eCommerce Manager | Migration tanked traffic, taking the blame | Post-migration recovery playbook | Aladdin Temp-Rite +25% organic |
| Owner / President (smaller distributors) | Competitors outrank them on core category terms | Category-page teardown + revenue gap | World Wide Art 404s -90% |

**Tone:** overwhelm, not inadequacy. The buyer is competent and under-resourced. "You can't hand-optimize 8,000 pages and shouldn't have to" beats "your SEO is bad."

**No em-dashes in outbound copy. Plain text. One ask per email.**

## 5. Channels and motion

| Channel | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cold email | Primary | Dedicated cold domains, 2 senders, 40–60 new contacts/sender/week. Batches, not blast — protects the ~18-person delivery team. |
| LinkedIn | Secondary | Connect + 2–3 value touches to same contacts, staggered. **Co-signed by / sent from Brian Dwyer** — his 27-year track record is an asset. |
| Audit landing page | Air cover | `/programmatic-seo-teardown` — retarget visitors, capture the ones not ready to reply. |
| Founder content | Slow burn | Dwyer already authors the blog; repurpose into 1 LinkedIn teardown post/week to warm the list. |

**The CTA is the offer, not a call.** Lead with the free **Programmatic SEO Teardown**: a 5-minute Loom walking through 3 concrete issues on their category/product templates with a dollar estimate of the traffic gap. Booking a call is step two.

### Outbound build (operational)

1. **Source:** BuiltWith or Store Leads (Adobe Commerce / BigCommerce / Shopify Plus) ∩ NAICS 423/424 (wholesale/distribution) + 31–33 (manufacturing) ∩ US ∩ revenue $10–250M.
2. **Enrich:** Ahrefs/Semrush 12-month organic trend; last-replatform date (BuiltWith history); headcount; marketing-team size (LinkedIn); recent leadership hires.
3. **Segment:**
   - **A — Post-migration drop** (replatformed + traffic down): recovery message. *Start here.*
   - **B — Decliners** (down >20% YoY, no migration): "core update or technical debt" message.
   - **C — Sleepers** (big catalog, flat organic, healthy paid spend): "money on the table" message.
4. **Personalize line 1:** name one real issue found on their site — noindexed facets, JS-rendered PDPs, thin category copy, redirect chains, missing product/breadcrumb schema, orphaned category pages.
5. **Cadence:** 4 emails over 18 days + 3 LinkedIn touches. Same-day teardown delivery on any positive reply.
6. **Qualify hard before the teardown:** platform, SKU count, revenue band, who owns SEO. Protects capacity.

## 11. Risks specific to this GTM

1. **Capacity.** Small team can't absorb a runaway campaign. → Batch sending, hard qualification, contractor bench for content/links.
2. **Key person.** Dwyer is the brand and likely the closer. → Systematize the teardown + audit so any senior can deliver; Dwyer only on closing calls.
3. **Category noise.** "eCommerce SEO agency" is crowded. → Never use that phrase in outbound; always lead with B2B/industrial + migration recovery + programmatic scale.
4. **Long sales cycle.** B2B distributors move slowly. → The paid audit is the near-term revenue; retainer is the compounding win.
