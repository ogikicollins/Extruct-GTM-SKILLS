## 1. What we are actually selling

InteractOne SEO is not "generic SEO." The sharp wedge is **technical and programmatic SEO for large-catalog B2B, industrial, and distribution eCommerce** running on Adobe Commerce, BigCommerce, or Shopify Plus.

Three things make it sellable cold:

1. **Catalog complexity.** Thousands of SKUs and category pages that never get optimized by hand. Programmatic SEO is a real capability most competitors do not have.
2. **Onshore, 28 years, dev + SEO under one roof.** They can diagnose a rendering or ERP-feed problem that a pure content agency cannot even see.
3. **Land-and-expand.** SEO is a low-risk first check that opens migration, CRO, and PPC work later.

Positioning line: *"The SEO team that reads your Magento logs, not just your blog."*

---

## 3. Trigger events (rank order for list building)

1. **Replatform / migration in last 6–12 months** — organic traffic almost always drops 20-40%, and nobody owns the recovery. Highest intent.
2. **Organic visibility decline >20% YoY** (Ahrefs / Semrush trend) with flat or growing paid spend.
3. **New Head of eCommerce / Marketing Director hire** (last 90 days) — new budget, wants a quick win.
4. **Job posting for SEO / Digital Marketing Manager** — they feel the gap and have not filled it.
5. **Site relaunch or redesign announced.**
6. **Google core update losers** (published third-party lists after each update).

## 5. Channels and motion

**Primary: cold email** with a personalized site-issue first line + free Loom audit CTA.
**Secondary: LinkedIn** — connect and 2-3 value touches to the same contacts, staggered with email.
**Air cover:** retargeting on the audit landing page; monthly "B2B SEO teardown" post on the InteractOne LinkedIn page.

**The CTA is the offer, not a call.** Lead with a **free Programmatic SEO Teardown**: a 5-minute Loom walking through 3 concrete issues on their category/product templates with a dollar estimate of the traffic gap. Booking a call is the second step.

### Outbound build

1. **Source:** BuiltWith or Store Leads (Adobe Commerce / BigCommerce / Shopify Plus) ∩ NAICS 423/424 (wholesale/distribution) + 33x (manufacturing) ∩ US ∩ revenue band.
2. **Enrich:** pull 12-month organic traffic trend (Ahrefs API / Semrush) and last-relaunch date.
3. **Segment:**
   - **A — Post-migration drop** (replatformed + traffic down): recovery message.
   - **B — Decliners** (down >20% YoY, no migration): "core update or technical debt" message.
   - **C — Sleepers** (big catalog, flat organic, healthy paid): "you are leaving money on the table" message.
4. **Personalize:** every first line names one real issue found on their site (missing category schema, noindexed facets, JS-rendered PDPs, thin category copy, canonical mess).
5. **Cadence:** 4 emails over 18 days + 3 LinkedIn touches. ~40-60 new contacts/week per sender, 2 senders per domain, dedicated cold domains.
