## Who is TopServ, and what do they sell

TopServ is a "Brandformance" agency — brand-first, video-first marketing for home service contractors. They do **not** sell leads or ads as a standalone service. Their pitch: help an already-established business become the name people already know in their market, before the customer ever starts shopping around, instead of paying more every year to be found in the moment of need.

**The program (never quote pricing on the SDR call — for context only):**
- BRAND Builder — $5,000/mo (floor)
- Accelerator — $8,000/mo
- Dominator — $10,000/mo
- + $10,000 one-time activation on every program
- Ad spend paid separately, direct to platforms
- Mandatory 2-day on-site video shoot for every client — core to the program, not optional. Don't dwell on it, but don't hide it if it comes up.

**Do not describe this as lead generation.** Their positioning is pure visibility/recognition — customers coming to the business inbound because they already know the name, not because of a paid click. If a prospect starts treating the call like a lead-gen pitch, redirect toward recognition/being known, not toward "we can get you more/cheaper leads."

## Who to call / who to skip

**Call:** HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing. Owner, President, Founder, or COO only — has to be someone who can unilaterally commit to a brand investment. Revenue $1.5M–$5M (sweet spot $2.5M–$5M). 3–5+ service techs is the real size signal, not revenue. Established 2–3+ years. Independently/privately owned.

**Skip:** Water restoration companies. Franchisees. Marketing coordinators or anyone without budget authority. Obviously price-led/"cheapest in town" operators. PE-backed rollups. Anything that reads as a broken operation on the call (that's a discovery-call judgment, not a list filter — flag it, don't try to save it).

## Call flow — no pitch, ever

TopServ's hard rule: **never pitch services, pricing, ROI, or guarantees on this call.** Your only job is to get a genuine, confirmed booking. No "How are you?" / "Is this a good time?" openers.

1. **Open:** "Hey [First Name], this is [Name] calling on behalf of TopServ — got 20 seconds?"
2. **Hook (universal trigger):** "Quick one — if someone within 5 miles of you needed a [trade] company six months from now, are they already calling you, or would they still be Googling around?"
3. **Discovery (one question, listen hard):** "How are you getting most of your new customers these days — mostly ads, referrals, or a mix?" *(Listen for: frustration with current/past agency — this is the single strongest signal, escalate immediately if you hear it.)*
4. **Bridge to booking, no pitch:** "That's actually exactly what our team works on — helping [trade] companies become the name people already know before they need you, instead of just showing up in a search that day. Worth a quick call with Jonathan/Ryan to see if there's a fit? What's easier, mornings or afternoons?"

## Objection bridges (acknowledge + redirect to the call — never resolve it yourself)

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "It's too expensive" | "Fair — worth noting what you're already paying Google every month doesn't build into anything. Jonathan/Ryan walks through the real numbers on the call." |

| "I don't want to be on camera" | Don't try to resolve it — "Totally fair, that's worth raising directly on the call." |
| "I need to think about it" | "Totally — just so I point you right, is it more about timing, budget, or looping someone else in?" |

## Vocabulary — always/never

- Say **"Brandformance"** — never "brand performance"
- Say **"program"** — never package, plan, or retainer
- Say **"lead capture"** — never "lead gen," when referencing paid ads

- Never commit to a number of leads, calls, or booked jobs, and never guarantee rankings or cost-per-lead

## Getting the first 5 on the board

- **Quality over volume, always.** A booking with no verbal yes to a specific date/time doesn't count — every meeting gets run through the call-quality review before it touches Jonathan/Ryan's calendar. A string of soft bookings stalls the scale gate even if the count looks fine.
- **Call windows that actually work for this audience:** 7–8am, 11:30am–1pm, 4–6pm — contractors are on job sites the rest of the day.
- **Suppress before you dial** — cross-check every list against TopServ's exclusion list and GHL segment first. Don't call anyone on it.
- **The fastest path to a booking is hearing frustration with a prior agency.** The moment you hear it, stop probing and go straight to the ask.
- 5 solid meetings this month is the goal — the real target behind it is 50/month by March, so build the habit of doing this right now, not just doing it fast.
