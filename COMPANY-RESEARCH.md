## Executive Summary

InteractOne is a 27-year-old, ~18-person, 100% US-based eCommerce agency in Cincinnati, Ohio, founded and run by Brian Dwyer. It has quietly served 250+ brands with an average client tenure of 5+ years, and has recently repositioned from a general "Magento/Shopify/BigCommerce agency" to **"B2B commerce built to perform"** — targeting manufacturers, distributors, and industrial/MRO sellers with complex catalogs (high SKU counts, fitment data, custom pricing, punchout, B2B portals).

The SEO service is a full-stack offering: technical SEO, SEO audits, keyword research, link building, content creation, programmatic SEO for large catalogs, and GA4/Looker Studio reporting. It is delivered by senior staff with no junior hand-off and no offshore. Case studies show real outcomes (Aladdin Temp-Rite +66% total / +25% organic traffic; BASCO +106% users / +73% revenue; World Wide Art bounce rate -88%, 404s -90%).

For an outbound motion, this is a **strong but under-marketed offer**. The differentiation (onshore senior team + developer DNA + B2B/industrial catalog expertise + programmatic SEO) is genuinely scarce, but the firm is small, has no public pricing, shows slightly declining headcount, and competes in a crowded "eCommerce SEO agency" category where buyers default to bigger names. The winning outbound play is narrow: **post-migration organic recovery and large-catalog programmatic SEO for B2B/industrial distributors on Adobe Commerce and BigCommerce**, led with a free technical teardown rather than a "book a call" CTA.

## Risks

2. **Small team, slight headcount decline** — limited capacity to absorb a successful campaign. *Mitigation: run outbound in controlled batches (40–60 contacts/week), qualify hard, and price to protect margin; consider a vetted contractor bench for content/links.*
3. **Crowded category, low brand awareness** — buyers anchor on bigger names. *Mitigation: never compete on "eCommerce SEO agency"; compete on "B2B/industrial large-catalog programmatic SEO + migration recovery," a category with few credible names.*
4. **No AEO / AI-search offering** — a 2026 buyer question and competitor wedge. *Mitigation: package a light AEO add-on (schema, entity optimization, answer-formatted content) at +$900–2,500/mo; add one line to the deck.*
