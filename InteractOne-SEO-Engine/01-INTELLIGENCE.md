## 1. Company context (the source of truth for all copy)

**One-liner:** InteractOne is a 27-year, 100% US-based eCommerce agency that runs technical and programmatic SEO for B2B and industrial retailers with large, complex catalogs on Adobe Commerce and BigCommerce.

**What we sell in this engine:** the SEO service line, entered through a free technical teardown and a fixed-fee audit, expanding into a monthly SEO retainer and then into InteractOne's core dev/migration/CRO work.

**Why we win (the four-way edge — use the intersection, never one leg alone):**
1. Developers and SEOs under one roof. We fix rendering, faceted nav, feed, and template problems, not just publish content.
2. Programmatic SEO for catalogs with thousands of SKUs and attribute combinations.
3. B2B/industrial fluency: fitment, custom pricing, punchout, account portals, technical product data.
4. Onshore senior delivery. No junior hand-off, no offshore, 5+ year average client tenure.

**Positioning line:** "The SEO team that reads your Magento logs, not just your blog."

### Voice rules
- Plain, direct, specific. Short sentences. No hype adjectives ("game-changing", "world-class").
- **No em-dashes.** Use a period or "and" or a comma.
- Lead with the prospect's situation, not our credentials. Credentials are the third beat, not the first.
- Name real numbers. "20 to 40 percent" not "a lot."
- Never use the phrase "eCommerce SEO agency" or "SEO services" as the pitch. The pitch is a specific outcome.
- One ask per email. The ask is almost always "want me to send the teardown video?"

## 2. Personas

### Persona A — The eCommerce Manager (post-migration pain)
- Titles: eCommerce Manager, Digital Commerce Manager, Online Store Manager, Web Manager
- Company: $10-80M B2B/industrial retailer that replatformed in the last 6-12 months
- Pain: organic sessions dropped after the migration, leadership is asking why, they do not have the technical depth or time to diagnose it
- Wants: the traffic back, a clear plan, someone who has done this exact recovery before
- Emotional state: exposed, a little defensive. Message to the problem, not to them.
- Entry hook: "your organic traffic after the [platform] move"

### Persona B — The Marketing Director / Head of Digital (catalog scale pain)
- Titles: Marketing Director, Director of Digital Marketing, Head of eCommerce, VP Marketing
- Company: $25-150M distributor/manufacturer, 5,000-100,000 SKUs, flat organic despite a big catalog
- Pain: a 1-3 person team cannot hand-optimize thousands of category and product pages; paid CAC keeps rising
- Wants: organic to scale with the catalog without hiring; less dependence on Google Ads
- Entry hook: "8,000 product pages, [N] ranking"

### Persona C — The VP / Director of eCommerce (revenue pain)
- Titles: VP eCommerce, Director of eCommerce, GM Digital, Head of DTC/B2B Commerce
- Company: $50-250M, board or exec pressure on the organic revenue line
- Pain: organic revenue is flat or declining while paid spend grows; competitors outrank them on core category terms
- Wants: a defensible organic channel, a number they can put in the board deck
- Entry hook: "organic revenue vs paid spend at [company]"

## 3. Pain hypotheses (H1-H6)

Each hypothesis = a detectable signal + a list angle + an email angle + the proof to cite. Track performance in Layer 6; graduate winners (>4% reply), retire losers (<2% over 50 sends).

### H1 — Post-migration organic collapse *(primary, start here)*
- **Belief:** Companies that replatformed in the last 6-12 months lost 20-40% of organic traffic to migration errors nobody owns.
- **Signal:** BuiltWith platform-change history shows a platform switch in the last year AND Ahrefs/Semrush organic traffic trend is down 15%+ since that date.
- **List angle:** platform changed in last 12 months + organic down.
- **Email angle:** "The drop is almost always a handful of technical misses in the migration, not a content problem."
- **Proof:** Aladdin Temp-Rite (+25% organic post Magento → Adobe Commerce).
- **Persona:** A.

### H2 — Large catalog, tiny SEO footprint
- **Belief:** Distributors with 5,000+ SKUs have thousands of category/attribute pages that were never optimized and do not rank.
- **Signal:** 5,000+ products indexed (site: query / crawl) BUT fewer than 10% of category URLs rank on page 1-2 for their head terms (Ahrefs).
- **List angle:** SKU count high + organic keywords low relative to page count.
- **Email angle:** "You have roughly [N] product and category pages and about [M] of them rank. Programmatic SEO is how you close that gap without hiring."
- **Proof:** World Wide Art crawl cleanup; InteractOne "scalable SEO for large catalogs".
- **Persona:** B.

### H3 — Paying to rent traffic they should own
- **Belief:** Companies with heavy Google Ads spend and flat organic are one core-update away from a CAC crisis.
- **Signal:** High paid keyword count / estimated paid traffic (Ahrefs/Semrush) AND flat-to-declining organic over 12 months.
- **List angle:** paid traffic >> organic traffic, organic trend flat.
- **Email angle:** "Every dollar of that Google Ads spend is rent. Here is what the organic version of your top 5 paid terms would cost to win once."
- **Proof:** BASCO (+73% revenue), note paid was part of the mix.
- **Persona:** C.

### H4 — Core update loser
- **Belief:** Sites that dropped on a recent Google core update have fixable content-quality and technical-trust issues.
- **Signal:** Visible ranking/traffic drop aligned to a published core-update date (third-party loss lists, Ahrefs).
- **List angle:** traffic step-down within 2 weeks of a core-update date.
- **Email angle:** "Your traffic stepped down right when the [month] core update rolled out. That pattern is fixable and we can tell you in 5 minutes whether it is content or technical."
- **Proof:** World Wide Art technical cleanup.
- **Persona:** B or A.

### H5 — Aging Magento, SEO as the wedge to a migration
- **Belief:** Companies on Magento 1 or early Magento 2 have compounding technical SEO debt; the SEO conversation opens the replatform conversation.
- **Signal:** BuiltWith shows Magento 1.x or Magento 2.0-2.3 (EOL/near-EOL).
- **List angle:** Magento version <= 2.3.
- **Email angle:** "Half the SEO problems on [site] trace back to the Magento version. We can fix the quick wins now and scope the platform question separately."
- **Proof:** Aladdin Temp-Rite migration; Adobe Bronze partner.
- **Persona:** A or B. *Route positive replies to InteractOne's core migration pipeline too.*

### H6 — New marketing leader, first 90 days
- **Belief:** A marketing/eComm leader hired in the last 90 days needs a visible early win and has fresh budget.
- **Signal:** LinkedIn job-change: Director+/VP Marketing or eCommerce started in last 90 days at an ICP-fit company.
- **List angle:** leadership start date < 90 days + ICP firmographics.
- **Email angle:** "You have probably already spotted that organic is underperforming. A teardown gives you a ranked list of what to fix first, which is a useful thing to have in month one."
- **Proof:** 27 years, 250+ brands (de-risk the vendor choice for a new leader).
- **Persona:** B or C.

## 4. Competitor map + displacement

| Competitor type | Names | Their weakness | Displacement angle |

| Do nothing | n/a | Lost rankings compound monthly | "Post-migration, every month makes the rankings harder to win back." |
