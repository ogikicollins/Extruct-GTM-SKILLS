# Layer 5 — Close

Goal: convert audit calls into paid audits, and paid audits into retainers. The paid audit is the near-term revenue and the qualifier. The retainer is the compounding win.

---

## 1. The two-step close

```
Teardown call  →  Paid Audit ($3.5-7.5k)  →  Retainer ($5-15k/mo)  →  Expansion (migration / CRO / PPC / AEO)
   free            fixed scope, 2-3 wks       month-to-month or 6-mo      InteractOne core business
```

Why audit-first: it is a small, low-risk yes for a cautious B2B buyer, it pays for the sales effort even if no retainer follows, and it surfaces the real scope so the retainer is priced right. **40-60% of paid audits should convert to a retainer** within 60 days of delivery.

---

## 2. Deal stages

| Stage | Entry condition | Exit condition | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 · Teardown Review held | Call happened | Audit proposed or check-in set | Rep |
| S2 · Audit proposed | Fixed-fee SOW sent | Audit signed or lost | Rep + Dwyer |
| S3 · Audit in delivery | SOW signed, paid | Audit readout call held | Delivery team |
| S4 · Retainer proposed | Readout done, roadmap delivered | Retainer signed or deferred | Dwyer |
| S5 · Won | Retainer signed | Onboarding starts (Layer 5 §6) | Delivery |
| S6 · Deferred | Not now, but real | Re-engage on trigger or at agreed date | Engine (Layer 6) |
| S7 · Lost | Clear no | Reason logged, hypothesis updated | Engine |

**Deal health:** score each open deal weekly on next-step-agreed (yes/no), champion identified (yes/no), economic buyer engaged (yes/no), 7+ days silent (flag). Two or more red = stall alert → Dwyer touches personally.

## 3. Post-call nurture sequence (sent from rep's personal inbox, not the sequencer)

**After S1 (teardown review), no audit signed same day:**
- Day 0: recap email. The 3 issues, the 2 extras shown live, the ranked fix list attached, the audit SOW attached, one line: "No pressure on the audit. The fix list is yours regardless."
- Day 3: one relevant proof point (the closest case study by vertical/platform).
- Day 7: a light check. "Did the fix list land with the team? Any questions on the audit scope?"
- Day 14: the cost-of-waiting angle specific to their segment (H1: rankings compound; H3: next core update).
- Day 21: breakup. "I will close this out. When the timing is right, the SOW is still good and the numbers only move one way."
- Then → S6 Deferred, re-engage rules in Layer 6.

**After S3 (audit delivered):** readout call is scripted to end with the retainer proposal on screen. Follow with:
- Day 1: proposal PDF + a 90-day projected-traffic chart.
- Day 4: "which of the three retainer scopes fits how you want to work" (anchoring with options, not yes/no).
- Day 8: offer a 60-day trial scope at the low tier to de-risk.
- Day 14: Dwyer personal note.

## 4. Proposal template (S2 audit SOW and S4 retainer)

**Audit SOW (1 page):**
```
InteractOne · Technical & Programmatic SEO Audit — {company}
Fee: ${3,500 / 5,500 / 7,500}  (based on {sku_count} SKUs, {platform})
Timeline: 2-3 weeks
Deliverables:
  - Full technical crawl + Core Web Vitals + rendering audit
  - Migration diff (for H1: old vs new URL/redirect/schema map)
  - Category & product template SEO review (programmatic opportunities)
  - Keyword-to-page gap analysis
  - Prioritized 90-day roadmap: quick wins (wk 1-2), structural (wk 3-8), content (ongoing)
  - 60-minute readout call
Ownership: every deliverable is yours. No lock-in.
```

**Retainer proposal (3 tiers):**
| Tier | Scope | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Recover | technical fixes + migration recovery + monthly reporting | $5,000/mo |
| Scale | Recover + programmatic template work + content (8-12 pages/mo) + link building | $9,500/mo |
| Compound | Scale + AEO/AI-search optimization + CRO testing + quarterly strategy | $15,000/mo |

6-month initial term, then month-to-month. AEO add-on to Recover/Scale: +$1,500/mo.

## 5. Objection bank

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We already have an SEO agency." | "Makes sense. Most agencies we replace are strong on content and thin on the technical and platform side. The teardown is free and it will tell you in 5 minutes whether your current setup has a technical gap. If it does not, you have lost nothing." |
| "We do SEO in-house." | "Good, that helps. The question is bandwidth. One person cannot template-optimize 8,000 pages or run a migration recovery on top of the day job. We often work alongside an in-house lead, not instead of them." |
| "Not the right time / no budget." | "Understood. Two things. The teardown is free, so take it now and use it whenever. And the fixed-fee audit is a one-time cost, not a retainer commitment, if you want a plan on the shelf for next quarter." |
| "Does SEO even matter with AI search?" | "It matters more. AI answers pull from structured, well-organized pages. The same technical work that fixes your rankings, schema, clean templates, crawlability, is what gets you cited in AI results. We package that as an AEO add-on." |
| "How do I know it will work?" | "You do not, yet. That is why the first step is a fixed-fee audit, not a 12-month retainer. You get the roadmap and decide. One B2B client recovered 25 percent organic in the first 90 days, but I will show you the plan before you commit to the ongoing work." |
| "You're a small agency." | "Deliberately. You get senior people who have done this for 27 years, and you are not account number 300 handed to a junior. Our average client stays 5-plus years." |
| "Send me some info." | "Better than info: I will record a teardown of your actual site. Three specific issues, dollar impact, yours to circulate. What is the best category page to start from?" |

## 6. Client onboarding (Day 0-90 after retainer signed)

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 0 | Kickoff call. **Capture before-state:** current organic sessions, current rankings for 20 head terms, GA4 access, Search Console access, current conversion rate. This is the baseline for the Day 90 proof story. |
| 1-5 | Access provisioning, crawl, quick-win list confirmed |
| 6-30 | Ship quick wins (redirects, indexation, schema, Core Web Vitals). First movement visible in Search Console. |
| 30 | Month-1 report vs baseline. First win captured (even a small one) and written up. |
| 31-60 | Structural work: template rebuilds, programmatic category pages, internal linking |
| 60 | Month-2 report. Ask for the first referral if health is green (see §8). |
| 61-90 | Content + link building cadence established, AEO scoping if applicable |
| 90 | Quarterly business review. Before/after numbers → case study draft → renewal conversation → expansion pitch (§7). |

**Client health score (weekly, 0-100):** results trend (30) + responsiveness (20) + scope stability (15) + sentiment logged after each call (20) + payment on time (15). Below 60 = Dwyer intervention.

---

## 7. Expansion paths (the real LTV)

Trigger the pitch at the Day 90 QBR and each subsequent QBR:
1. **Migration** — if on Magento 1/2.3 or fighting the platform. InteractOne's core business. Largest deal size.
2. **CRO** — once organic traffic is up, "let's convert more of it." Natural follow-on.
3. **PPC** — take over the paid program to coordinate with organic (avoid cannibalization, share negatives).
4. **AEO** — AI-search optimization as a standalone or add-on.
5. **PIM / feed / integration work** — surfaces during the audit, hand to InteractOne's dev team.

## 8. Referral engine

- **When:** client health green at Day 60+, or right after a win is captured.
- **Ask (from Dwyer or the rep, personal):** "You mentioned {peer company / association}. The teardown offer works for referrals too. Would you be comfortable intro-ing me to {name}, or sharing the teardown idea with them?"
- **Referral leads skip cold sequencing.** Enter at Layer 4 WARM with a warm-path bonus. Close rate 2-3x cold.
- Log every referral as its own pipeline source in `engine/referrals.md`.
